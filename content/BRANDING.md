# Studio identity context

BLACKGLASS is the umbrella organization; Blackglass Studios is the game-development identity. Tagline: “Build Worlds Beyond.” Working studio motif: three collaborators / three shards of black glass forming an impossible infinite loop.

This is branding context, not raeon Black rank or Black Mode. The pre-existing BLACKGLASS technical project donated the name; its technical architecture is not imported or made a raeon dependency. No gameplay authority follows from branding.

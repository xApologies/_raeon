# Hidden Black Mode

GAME_CANON: constructing/possessing a legal all-Black 60-card deck (3 Black Primes + 57 Black Utilities) unlocks hidden Black Mode. Black cards are also legal in otherwise ordinary decks. [Black rules](../cards/BLACK_RANK.md) and [accepted state](../../data/manifests/accepted-state.json) preserve these distinctions.

PROVISIONAL / accepted product direction: this is an Easter egg and long-term mastery reward, potentially discovered after extended casual play. Do not present it as an ordinary visible onboarding progress bar. It is an extreme/endgame difficulty around a full Black deck; an opponent may use the same Black ecosystem.

Challenge comes from Black-card power, deck construction, strategic play and mastery of the same systems. The AI must not cheat through hidden game/QMO violations. Exact Black AI behavior remains OPEN. This is later-production design direction, not an implemented mode. Source: [Block 2](../../provenance/sources/recovery-0003-block-2.txt).

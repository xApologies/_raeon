# Economy and shop direction

Authority: PROVISIONAL — current design direction from the [recovery source](../../provenance/sources/recovery-0003-block-2.txt); machine-readable direction is in [accepted state](../../data/manifests/accepted-state.json).

Players earn progression/credits through play. The current direction does not require purchasing gameplay credits. Do not introduce player-to-player credit wagering. PvP may reward game-generated progression/credits; exact rewards remain OPEN. Long-term duplicate collection may contribute to difficult/expensive Black-card endgame crafting. Later Cycles may be released as content.

OPEN: recipes, credit values, pack probabilities, economy rates, exact PvP rewards, release pricing, and transaction/implementation details. No monetization implementation or final pricing is introduced. See [collection/progression](../progression/README.md).


Current release direction favors inexpensive, player-friendly releases and later Cycles, with no subscription-dependent gameplay or pay-to-win gameplay-credit purchasing. No purchasable credit bundles are specified. Exact store prices remain OPEN; no numerical price is canonized. Future commercial changes require explicit approval.

Intended loop: play → game-generated progression/credits → acquire/open cards → build collection. PvP rewards come from the game, never wagers of existing player balances. Pack sizes, probabilities, recipes, costs and rates remain OPEN.

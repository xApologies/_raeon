## Color transport and authority

Reuse **Rainbow Road**, the Genesis Horizon's existing information/color transport. No second board, attack or defense bus is introduced. Conceptual flow is resolved Field/Emergent source → Rainbow Road → Prime → admitted target → Nexus-mediated State transformation. Rainbow Road supplies transport; Prime family and current [ACTIVE/DEFENSE authority](../game/match/SYSTEM.md) determine permitted operations.

Ordinary Configuration Fields do not directly make hostile cross-board attacks. Current field color supplies the owner's Prime system. ACTIVE permits friendly Restore and hostile Degrade; DEFENSE permits friendly Restore only, including Universal's Restore direction, with no Degrade counterattack. A healthy charged Prime can spend partial C repeatedly while it remains available. Neither transport nor arithmetic overrides QMO admission, unresolved targeting/timing, or missing broader transduction mathematics.

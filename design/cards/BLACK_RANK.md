# Black-rank design

Authority: GAME_CANON for explicit Black rules; PROVISIONAL / WORKING DESIGN for the two named concepts. Sources: [Block 2](../../provenance/sources/recovery-0003-block-2.txt), [Block 3](../../provenance/sources/recovery-0003-block-3.txt). Structured authority: [accepted state](../../data/manifests/accepted-state.json).

Black is rank/charge 8, above White. It applies to Prime Fields and Utilities. **BLACK FIELD GENERATORS DO NOT EXIST**; White is the highest Generator rank. Exactly three Black Prime identities exist. Black Utilities form a larger endgame catalog outside the ordinary Cycle-1 50-Utility set; that catalog is OPEN and no final printed cards are created here.

Black cards may occur in normal constructed and PvP decks, independently of Black Mode. Their intended cost/difficulty is in long-term in-game progression and crafting. Exceptional efficiency or bypassing ordinary setup is allowed; violating game mechanics or QMO legality is not. The resulting game state must be legal.

## Shield of the Abyss — working concept

A Black Utility concept provides a Black shield, charge 8, including to a lower-ranked Prime. A Yellow Prime may receive the exceptional shield while remaining its original Prime identity. The shield is the Black effect, not a change of Prime identity. Final wording, timing, cost and printed-card status remain OPEN.

## Topaz Lake — working concept

A Black Utility concept directly manifests an already-defined legal Yellow QMO field/manifold state instead of requiring ordinary Generator setup. It bypasses setup economy, never makes illegal geometry legal. Exact target QMO, timing, lifetime and implementation remain OPEN. No QMO definition is fabricated.

## Three support Primes and the Fourth Prime

The three actual Black Prime cards occupy the deck. FourthPrimeAvailable iff BlackPrime1.alive AND BlackPrime2.alive AND BlackPrime3.alive. The Fourth Prime is emergent/supported, not a fourth deck card. Any support death makes it **inaccessible**, not necessarily destroyed. If future legal mechanics restore all three, reevaluate the condition; this grants no ordinary resurrection mechanic. Detailed ability remains OPEN.

A legal all-Black deck is 3 Black Primes + 57 Black Utilities = 60, with no Generators. See [Black Mode](../progression/BLACK_MODE.md). Final catalog, balance, implementation, exact timing and special costs remain unvalidated.

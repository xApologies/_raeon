
# Hand, Setup, Draw and First-Player Contract

- Deck: 60 cards.
- Opening draw: 7 ordinary Deck cards per player.
- Hand maximum: 10.
- Over-cap admission is atomic rejection until overflow law is defined.
- Mulligan exists; exact procedure remains OPEN.
- First player: fair binary outcome analogous to coin toss; record authoritative result. Exact PRNG OPEN.
- Second player: after opening draw, receive one extra non-Deck single-use Draw-1 compensation card in Hand; zero resource cost; counts toward capacity 10; may be held. Final name/ID/flavor/timing OPEN.
- Normal own-turn draw: 1 top Deck card.
- Extra draw requires admitted card effects.

8. SANDBOX ACTIVATION UTILITIES — 6
======================================================================

This family was previously called Sandbox / Geometry.

Use the clearer concept:

    SANDBOX ACTIVATION

A Sandbox is an available configuration space.

Each player begins with three permanent universal Sandbox Domains.

The three permanent starting Sandboxes are NOT restricted by the temporary
colored-capacity system below.

The six Utility-created Sandbox identities are:

    Red
    Orange
    Yellow
    Green
    Blue
    Violet

Their rank determines MAXIMUM FIELD GENERATOR CAPACITY:

    Red Sandbox    -> 3 Field Generator capacity
    Orange Sandbox -> 4
    Yellow Sandbox -> 5
    Green Sandbox  -> 6
    Blue Sandbox   -> 7
    Violet Sandbox -> 8

Formally:

    Capacity(SandboxColor) = ColorCharge + 2

for Red through Violet.

Examples:

A Red temporary Sandbox may contain at most 3 committed Field Generator cards.

A fourth Field Generator cannot be committed to that Red Sandbox even if doing
so would otherwise produce interesting geometry.

A Violet temporary Sandbox may contain at most 8.

CRITICAL:

Sandbox capacity does NOT guarantee a manifold.

For example:

    Violet Sandbox capacity = 8

does NOT imply:

    Violet manifold exists.

The participating Field Generator configuration must still pass QMO closure.

The Sandbox merely provides bounded configuration space.

Normal Utility copy limits apply.

Therefore a deck may contain, for example:

    Red Sandbox
    Red Sandbox
    Yellow Sandbox
    Blue Sandbox

provided no Utility identity exceeds the normal maximum of 3 copies.

There is NO rule saying only one Sandbox card of each color may appear in a deck.

Temporary Sandbox destruction/expiry details should remain OPEN unless already
canonically defined elsewhere.

10. GRAVEYARD / RECOVERY UTILITIES — 6
======================================================================

Recovery moves cards from Graveyard back into usable circulation.

Recovery does not automatically reconstruct a destroyed manifold.

Recovered Field Generators must be used normally to construct new legal topology.

Accepted six-card structural set:

1. Return 1 Field Generator from Graveyard to hand.
   Working rank: Yellow.

2. Return 2 Field Generators from Graveyard to hand.
   Working rank: Blue.

3. Return 3 Field Generators from Graveyard to hand.
   Working rank: White.

4. Return 1 Utility from Graveyard to hand.
   Working rank: Green.

5. Return any 1 non-Prime card from Graveyard to hand.
   Working rank: Blue.

6. Return up to 3 cards from Graveyard to the top of the deck in any chosen order.
   Working rank: Violet.

Prime recovery/resurrection is NOT part of this ordinary six-card Recovery family.

Do not invent ordinary Prime resurrection.

The exact ranks here are current accepted working design; note that for Recovery,
rank primarily expresses rarity/power rather than direct color magnitude.

12. EMERGENT FIELD TARGETING RULE
======================================================================

Clarify this accepted rule in appropriate design/game documentation:

Emergent Fields cannot be directly attacked or destroyed.

They exist because required support manifolds/configurations maintain them.

The opponent removes an Emergent Field by breaking its required support structure.

If:

    M_A + M_B -> E

and a required support is destroyed:

    M_A -> destroyed

then:

    E -> inaccessible / gone

according to the support relation.

This is why a proposed "Coupling Stabilizer" Utility was rejected.

Do NOT create such a card in the ordinary Cycle-1 Utility catalog.

Emergent Amplification occupies that sixth Stability slot instead.

# PROVENANCE / AUTHORITY

Authority levels used by this package:

ESTABLISHED
    Explicitly stated by Genesis and accepted in the current workshop.

RECOVERED
    Present in the earlier raeon checkpoint/archive and not contradicted by current design.

PROVISIONAL
    A workshop recommendation or implementation convenience not yet adopted.

EXAMPLE_ONLY
    Used to explain mechanics; not a card, cost, reward, or balance value.

OPEN
    Deliberately unresolved.

Important recovery rules
------------------------
1. Do not merge superseded historical physical-marker rules into the mobile model.
2. Preserve the semantic distinction:
       physical square marker -> digital activation/transfer interaction
       physical pyramid marker -> digital Prime/output interaction
   without requiring literal digital markers.
3. Do not turn illustrative Utility cards into canon.
4. Do not turn illustrative Credit values or shop prices into canon.
5. Do not infer a fixed Generator/Utility deck ratio.
6. Do not infer topology memberships for FG-001..FG-120 until explicitly designed.
7. Keep AERA/Genesis/Chirality source mathematics distinct from the simplified game abstraction.
8. Sandbox provenance never substitutes for actual Generator-geometry validation.
9. Color is the shared ordinal state language, but its semantic role depends on object:
       manifold = current field state/strength,
       Prime health = intrinsic durability,
       Prime shield = expendable active transduction reservoir,
       Utility color semantics = not yet finalized,
       rarity color = collection tier, potentially separate from mechanical color.

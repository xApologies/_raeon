2. Derived fusion and emergent QMOs are generated internally.
3. Failed relations are stored explicitly.
4. Generator count sets color only after closure.
5. Sandbox provenance does not determine topology.
6. Field Generators committed to a sandbox cannot be extracted individually.
7. Fusion and emergent coupling are different operators.
8. The QMO is the mathematical object.
9. Rendered meshes are derived representations.
10. The Cycle generator must preserve nontrivial negative space; do not link everything.
11. A future Cycle should be reproducible from source math + this Constitution + its Amendment.
12. If a source law is unresolved, do not pretend the research corpus solved it. A game rule may be introduced, but label it GAME_CANON.

17 — DRAW / DECK UTILITIES: 7
======================================================================

The Draw / Deck family contains exactly seven Utility identities.

This family is intentionally NOT a simple Red-through-White ladder.

It contains:

    3 raw Draw effects
    3 information/selection effects
    1 hand-cycling effect

The accepted structural set is:

--------------------------------------------------
17A — DRAW 1
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Draw 1 card.

Working rank:

    Orange

This is direct card advantage.

--------------------------------------------------
17B — DRAW 2
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Draw 2 cards.

Working rank:

    Green

--------------------------------------------------
17C — DRAW 3
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Draw 3 cards.

Working rank:

    Violet

Cycle 1 ordinary raw Draw Utilities should not exceed Draw 3 without a later
explicit design amendment.

--------------------------------------------------
17D — SURVEY
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Look at the top 3 cards of the deck.
    Put those cards back in any order.

Working rank:

    Yellow

This changes information/order.

It does NOT create direct card advantage.

Hand count change:

    0

--------------------------------------------------
17E — SELECTION
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Look at the top 3 cards of the deck.
    Choose 1.
    Put the chosen card into the player's hand.
    Put the other 2 on the bottom of the deck.

Working rank:

    Green

This provides constrained selection.

It is intentionally NOT:

    search the entire deck for an exact card.

--------------------------------------------------
17F — EXCHANGE
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Discard any number of cards from the player's hand.
    Draw the same number of cards.

Working rank:

    Blue

If n cards are discarded:

    hand -> graveyard = n
    deck -> hand = n

Net hand-card-count change:

    0

This improves hand quality without generating raw card advantage.

It intentionally creates interaction with Graveyard / Recovery strategies.

--------------------------------------------------
17G — DEEP SURVEY
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Look at the top 7 cards of the deck.
    Choose 1.
    Put the chosen card into the player's hand.
    Shuffle the remaining 6 back into the deck.

Working rank:

    White

This is a high-power selection effect.

It is still not an unrestricted exact-card tutor.

--------------------------------------------------
17H — DRAW / DECK DOMAIN BOUNDARY
--------------------------------------------------

Draw / Deck effects manipulate:

    deck
    hand
    future deck ordering
    selection from the deck

They do NOT retrieve cards from the Graveyard.

Graveyard retrieval belongs to:

    Recovery

The exact maximum hand size is NOT finalized by this checkpoint.

Earlier discussion considered a small bounded hand, but do not silently canonize a
number unless the repository already contains an explicitly accepted value.

Likewise, exact excess-draw/overflow behavior remains OPEN unless already accepted.

18 — GRAVEYARD / RECOVERY UTILITIES: 6
======================================================================

Exactly six Recovery identities exist in Cycle 1.

Recovery moves previously spent/discarded/destroyed card resources from the
Graveyard back toward usable circulation.

Recovery does NOT automatically reconstruct destroyed topology.

If Field Generator cards belonging to a destroyed manifold are recovered, the player
must construct a new mathematically legal configuration normally.

Accepted structural set:

--------------------------------------------------
18A — RECOVER ONE FIELD GENERATOR
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Return 1 Field Generator card from Graveyard to hand.

Working rank:

    Yellow

--------------------------------------------------
18B — RECOVER TWO FIELD GENERATORS
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Return 2 Field Generator cards from Graveyard to hand.

Working rank:

    Blue

--------------------------------------------------
18C — RECOVER THREE FIELD GENERATORS
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Return 3 Field Generator cards from Graveyard to hand.

Working rank:

    White

--------------------------------------------------
18D — RECOVER ONE UTILITY
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Return 1 Utility card from Graveyard to hand.

Working rank:

    Green

--------------------------------------------------
18E — RECOVER ANY ONE NON-PRIME CARD
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Return any 1 non-Prime card from Graveyard to hand.

Working rank:

    Blue

Prime cards are explicitly excluded from this ordinary Recovery effect.

--------------------------------------------------
18F — RECOVER THREE TO DECK
--------------------------------------------------

Effect:

    Choose up to 3 eligible cards in the Graveyard.
    Put them on top of the player's deck in any chosen order.

Working rank:

    Violet

This is recovery without immediate direct card advantage.

It intentionally combos with Draw / Deck effects.

Example:

    Recover 3 to top of deck
        ->
    Draw 3

is a valid strategic combination if the player has the required cards/effects.

--------------------------------------------------
18G — PRIME RECOVERY
--------------------------------------------------

Ordinary Cycle-1 Recovery Utilities do NOT provide Prime resurrection/recovery.

Do not invent ordinary Prime resurrection during this update.

Future:

    White specialty
    Black effect
    later Cycle mechanic

may potentially explore it, but no such rule is accepted here.

--------------------------------------------------
18H — RECOVERY RANK INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------

For Recovery cards, card color/rank primarily represents:

    rarity
    strategic strength
    card power

It does NOT necessarily represent direct generated color magnitude.

19E — STRUCTURAL ANCHOR
--------------------------------------------------

Working name:

    Structural Anchor

Effect:

The next otherwise-lethal degradation event against the protected Local Manifold
does NOT destroy that manifold.

Instead:

    manifold survives at Red
    charge = 1

State transition:

    otherwise lethal
        ->
    Red

instead of:

    otherwise lethal
        ->
    destroyed

This preserves an already-valid topology.

It does not generate new closure.

It does not make an invalid QMO relationship legal.

Exact rank/timing remains OPEN unless already accepted elsewhere.

--------------------------------------------------
19F — EMERGENT AMPLIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------

Working name:

    Emergent Amplification

Effect:

    Increase one currently active Emergent Field by +1 color charge.

Examples:

    Red    -> Orange
    Orange -> Yellow
    Yellow -> Green
    Green  -> Blue
    Blue   -> Violet

Ordinary Cycle-1 Emergent Amplification is capped at:

    Violet
    charge 6

If an Emergent Field is already Violet, this ordinary effect does not create an
ordinary White or Black Emergent Field.

Do not invent overflow behavior beyond the cap.

This effect:

- does NOT create a new Emergent Field
- does NOT alter QMO closure
- does NOT alter support identity
- does NOT make the Emergent Field directly targetable
- does NOT preserve the Emergent Field after required support disappears

The field remains governed by its original support relationship.

20 — REJECTED COUPLING STABILIZER
======================================================================

Record the design reasoning where appropriate.

A proposed:

    Coupling Stabilizer

was rejected from the ordinary Cycle-1 Utility catalog.

Reason:

Emergent Fields are already not independently attackable/destructible.

They disappear when their required supporting manifold relationship is broken.

Creating a card whose job is merely to protect the Emergent Field directly would
conflict with / redundantly complicate that support model.

The sixth Stability slot is therefore:

    Emergent Amplification

not Coupling Stabilizer.

Do not create Coupling Stabilizer as an ordinary Cycle-1 card.

    Black Utility

25 — BLACK DECK / BLACK MODE
======================================================================

Black cards may be mixed into ordinary legal decks.

There is no rule requiring Black cards to be used only in Black Mode.

A player may encounter another human player who has one or more Black cards in an
otherwise ordinary deck.

The long-term hidden achievement is constructing a fully Black legal deck.

A full Black deck contains:

    3 Black Prime cards
    57 Black Utility cards
    ----------------------
    60 cards

There are no Black Field Generators in the full Black deck.

The existence of this deck intentionally transforms the normal construction
economy because Black Utilities can directly manipulate/manifest legal field states.

When a player successfully possesses/constructs a legal full Black deck:

    unlock hidden BLACK MODE

This is intended as an Easter egg / long-term mastery reward.

It should NOT be presented as an ordinary visible progression bar in normal player
onboarding.

Design intent:

A player might play raeon casually for a long period before discovering the hidden
all-Black condition.

======================================================================

    deck construction
    Field Generator placement
    Sandbox configuration

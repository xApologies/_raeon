# Configuration Space Constitution

Configuration Space is the persistent bounded container; field/manifold
is its current resolved state. A space owns identity, capacity,
committed FG contents, XY positions, 3D orientations,
relationship/closure state, and current resolved field QMO if any.

Temporary capacity is a maximum, not a construction requirement:
R3/O4/Y5/G6/B7/V8. A Violet space may hold 3 FGs and resolve a Red field
if those three close. Sandbox color = maximum FG occupancy; field color
= current closure.

Resolved spaces are mutable/reconfigurable. Add another FG to a resolved
space -\> prior field destabilizes to CONFIGURING -\> player may
reposition/reorient contents -\> a new admitted closure may resolve.
Example Violet space: 3 FG Red -\> add fourth -\> reconfigure -\> 4 FG
Orange if admitted.

Committed individual FGs still cannot be independently
extracted/transferred unless a future explicit mechanic allows.
Reconfiguration within their domain is allowed.

Strategic view: EMPTY dormant; CONFIGURING unresolved animated; RESOLVED
stable active field. Tap space for detailed configuration; exit returns
to strategic view without duplicate state.

# Ordinary Prime Field Constitution

There are **14 unique ordinary Prime identities**, copy limit 1, with
three active Prime positions.

Restore: Yellow +3, Green +4, Blue +5, Violet +6, White +7. (5) Degrade:
Yellow -3, Green -4, Blue -5, Violet -6, White -7. (5) Universal: Red
±1, Orange ±2, Yellow ±3, Green ±4. (4)

For rank r: intrinsic maximum health `H_max=r`; active
color-charge/shield maximum `C_max=r`. Full health/full charge gives
effective durability 2r without changing rank. Prime identity/rank never
changes with current charge.

Friendly Restore resolves intrinsic health first, then excess becomes
active charge. Example Green at (H,C)=(1,0) receives +4 -\> (4,1). A
Prime must be at full intrinsic health before excess Restore becomes
active charge.

Active charge is spendable partially. Green charge 4 spending 2 leaves
charge 2 (Orange). Restore Primes spend charge only positively; Degrade
only negatively; Universal either direction. Universal ceiling is Green.

Active charge is shield/overhealth. Hostile degradation hits C first,
then H. Example (4,2) receiving -3 -\> (3,0). At H=0 Prime becomes
INACTIVE, remains in its fixed Prime position, and is not sent to
Graveyard.

Black Primes remain separate/future. Exact turn/timing windows remain
for match-state design.

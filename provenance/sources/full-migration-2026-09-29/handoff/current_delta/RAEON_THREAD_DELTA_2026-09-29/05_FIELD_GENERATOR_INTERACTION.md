# Field Generator Interaction --- 2D Placement + 3D Orientation

FG is a CARD in Deck/Hand/Graveyard. When committed into a Configuration
Space it becomes its deployed Chirality/QMO orientation object. Concept:
`CARD -> deployed FG -> configuration -> resolved field`.

Fixed top-down interaction view. Each deployed FG has 2D position
`(x,y)` and 3D orientation `R`. No player-controlled positional Z and no
orbit-camera/CAD navigation. Constitutional rule: **2D placement + 3D
orientation**.

Imported FG data supplies occupancy/void, rotation_class,
chirality_parity, X-/X+/Y-/Y+/Z-/Z+ face states/color views,
compatibility and manifold membership. Interaction maps to that data; do
not invent replacement mathematics.

Controls remain simple: select, move in XY, orient/rotate. Exact
touch/mouse gesture tuning is implementation detail. Continuous-looking
motion may snap/magnetize to deterministic admitted states.

One FG alone forms no field. With two, compatible relationships may
appear. Partial useful relationships can produce faint
lines/particles/growing topology, stronger coherence and magnetic
snapping/hysteresis. This is presentation only and cannot invent
legality.

With 3--8 FGs, complexity comes from arrangement, not new controls.
After admitted closure: 3 Red, 4 Orange, 5 Yellow, 6 Green, 7 Blue, 8
Violet. Count alone never guarantees closure. Exact admitted state -\>
CONFIGURING to RESOLVED and manifold visually stabilizes.

Learning-through-play goal: orientation, chirality, relationships,
topology, closure and manifolds are learned by interaction rather than
required formal instruction.

# Configuration Space Merge / Fusion / Emergence

Merge is simple board interaction. Bring two Configuration Spaces
together; if admitted they snap/merge into one. Content composition:
`C_A(m FG)+C_B(n FG)->C_AB((m+n) FG)`. Strategic cost: two independent
spaces become one.

This is a tempo/board-width tradeoff. Example 3-FG + 4-FG spaces -\> one
7-FG combined domain; those seven may preserve/ snap to an admitted
topology or be reoriented into an admitted seven-FG Blue field. Count
alone does not guarantee it.

Sandbox Utilities buy back/add Configuration Space economy.

Do not invent a merged-domain capacity ceiling if source/current rules
do not define one; preserve composition and flag the exact
ceiling/runtime admission for explicit follow-up if necessary.

The 343 fusion-derived QMOs are lookup-driven results, not a new
minigame. Player manipulates spaces; QMO determines admitted result;
presentation animates snap/merge.

The 1,691 Emergent Fields arise automatically from admitted
coupling/relationships. Supports remain distinct:
`M_A + M_B -> M_A + M_B + E`. E requires no separate FG construction,
remains support-dependent/non-independently targetable under existing
canon, and is represented by field animation.

Only FG configuration is mechanically demanding. Downstream
fusion/emergence is deterministic composition/lookup.

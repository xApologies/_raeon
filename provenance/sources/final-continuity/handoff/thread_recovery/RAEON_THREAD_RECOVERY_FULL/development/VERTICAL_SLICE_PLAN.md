# First Playable Vertical Slice

Do not wait for all 200 cards.

After foundational turn/match rules, enough Prime design, Utility
completion, and representative QMO import, define a slice with: -
complete match from launch to win/loss; - three permanent Sandboxes; -
representative Field Generator subset; - representative
Red/Orange/Yellow closure paths; - 3--6 representative Primes; - 10--15
representative Utilities; - at least one fusion or emergent
relationship; - Prime activation/transduction; - representative manifold
rendering; - basic AI opponent; - basic UI; - save/load; - regression
coverage.

The slice must obey the real game/QMO rules even though content is
reduced.

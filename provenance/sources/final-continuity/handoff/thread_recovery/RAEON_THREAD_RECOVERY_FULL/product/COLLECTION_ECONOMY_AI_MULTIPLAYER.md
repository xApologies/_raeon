# Product / Collection / Economy / AI / Multiplayer

Long-term card collection is intended. Players earn game-generated
progression/credits through play and may use them in an in-game card
shop/pack loop. Duplicates retain value and are intended to support
future Black-card crafting.

Exact crafting recipes, probabilities, pack size, credit costs, reward
rates, and commercial pricing remain OPEN. No player-to-player credit
wagering. PvP may grant game-generated rewards. Product direction favors
player-friendly inexpensive releases, no subscription-dependent
gameplay, and no pay-to-win gameplay-credit purchasing. Future card
Cycles are intended.

Computer opponents with multiple difficulty levels are intended. Future
adaptive/player-modeling AI is a direction only; no ML architecture is
selected. AI must obey game/QMO legality.

Local peer-to-peer direction: one device may host match state, another
joins, devices exchange compact actions/state, and each renders locally.
A central gameplay server is not required for every local match.
Transport technology remains OPEN. Black cards are legal in ordinary PvP
decks. Matchmaking/balance remains OPEN.

# Prime Fields --- 30

Thirty unique ordinary Prime identities; three active Prime positions.

Primes are critical health-bearing and offensive/transduction
interfaces.

Working conceptual model: - intrinsic/base health-color; - active color
may form shield/overhealth; - incoming degradation interacts with
protection before intrinsic health where final rules specify; - active
color may be spent offensively, creating offense-versus-defense
decisions; - damaged intrinsic health may need restoration before excess
incoming color becomes shield; - higher-ranked Primes should generally
support greater transduction/shield capability.

Major missing work: actual 30-card catalog, rank/health distribution,
identity differentiation, passive/active/ triggered behavior if any,
targets, timing, shield/transduction constraints, rules text, and
machine operators.

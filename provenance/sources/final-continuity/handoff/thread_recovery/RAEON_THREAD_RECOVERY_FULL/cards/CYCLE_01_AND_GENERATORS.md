# Cycle 1 / Field Generators

Cycle 1 contains 200 ordinary cards: 120 Field Generators, 30 Prime
Fields, 50 Utilities. Deck size 60. FG identity max 2, Utility identity
max 3, Prime unique.

## Field Generators

There are 120 identities. They are mathematical/configurational cards
used to construct manifolds in Sandboxes. They should derive from
Chirality Fabric / Propagation Engine / QMO data, not be manually
reinvented. Expected eventual IDs: FG-001 through FG-120.

The eventual authoritative catalog should carry QMO identity,
chirality/configuration data, legal relationships, closure
participation, render references, and human-facing card metadata.

Maximum Field Generator rank is White. Black Field Generators do not
exist. Authoritative 120-card QMO catalog is SOURCE_IMPORT_REQUIRED.

# Black System

Black = rank/charge 8, above White. Applies to Primes and Utilities
only. No Black Field Generators. Black cards are legal in ordinary decks
and are intended as difficult/expensive crafted endgame objects. They
may bypass ordinary setup/efficiency but must still produce legal QMO
states.

Exactly three Black Prime identities exist. FourthPrimeAvailable iff
BlackPrime1.alive AND BlackPrime2.alive AND BlackPrime3.alive. The
Fourth Prime is emergent/supported, not a deck card, and becomes
inaccessible when any support Prime dies.

Full Black deck: 3 Black Primes + 57 Black Utilities = 60. It unlocks
hidden Black Mode.

Working concepts: - Shield of the Abyss: Black/8 shield, including on
lower-ranked Prime; Prime identity unchanged. - Topaz Lake: directly
manifests an already-defined legal Yellow field/manifold, bypassing
ordinary setup.

Both named concepts remain provisional. Final Black Utility catalog and
Fourth Prime ability remain OPEN.

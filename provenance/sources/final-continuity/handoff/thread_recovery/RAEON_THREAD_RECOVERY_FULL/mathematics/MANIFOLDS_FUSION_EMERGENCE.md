DerivedLocalManifoldQMO. Emergence: M_A + M_B -\> M_A + M_B + E when
admitted. Supports remain.

color pairing is legal. Explicit termination prevents everything from

remove/inactivate them. Emergent Amplification may add +1 charge up to

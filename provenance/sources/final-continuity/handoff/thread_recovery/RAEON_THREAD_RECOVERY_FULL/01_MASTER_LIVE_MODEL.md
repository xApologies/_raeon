# Master Live Model

raeon is a strategic card game built around mathematical configuration,
topology, color/charge transduction, Sandbox construction, Field
Generators, Local Manifolds, Prime Fields, fusion, emergent fields, and
collection.

Cycle 1 = 120 Field Generators + 30 Prime Fields + 50 Utilities = 200
ordinary cards. Deck size 60. Field Generator identity max 2 copies;
Utility identity max 3; Prime identity unique; 3 active Prime positions.

Color/charge: none=0, Red=1, Orange=2, Yellow=3, Green=4, Blue=5,
Violet=6, White=7, Black=8. White is maximum Field Generator rank. Black
Field Generators do not exist.

Each player begins with three permanent universal Sandbox Domains. Field
Generators are committed into Sandboxes. An individual committed
Generator cannot simply be extracted/moved; whole Sandbox Domains may
merge/fuse. Temporary Sandbox capacities: Red3, Orange4, Yellow5,
Green6, Blue7, Violet8. Permanent Sandboxes are universal. Capacity
never guarantees closure.

A Local Manifold exists only after QMO closure. After closure:
3/4/5/6/7/8 Generators correspond to
Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet. Arithmetic does not authorize
topology.

Base manifold basis: 15 Red + 13 Orange + 11 Yellow + 9 Green + 7 Blue +
5 Violet = 60. External inventory: 60 base + 343 fusion-derived + 1,691
Emergent = 2,094 render-relevant QMOs. Actual authoritative objects
remain SOURCE_IMPORT_REQUIRED.

Fusion: M_i ⊕ M_j -\> M\* when admitted. Emergence: M_A + M_B -\> M_A +
M_B + E; supports remain. Emergent Fields are support-bound,
non-targetable, and disappear/become inaccessible when support breaks.

Primes: 30 unique ordinary identities, 3 active positions. They are
health/offense/transduction interfaces. Working model: intrinsic
health/color plus possible active shield/color; active color may be
spent offensively. Actual 30-card Prime catalog remains a major design
task.

All 50 ordinary Utility structural slots are accounted for. Black = 8,
applies to Primes/Utilities only. Exactly 3 Black Prime identities. Full
Black deck = 3 Black Primes + 57 Black Utilities, unlocking Black Mode.
Fourth Prime is supported/emergent and available only while all three
Black Primes remain alive.

Rendering: QMO -\> deterministic RenderSpec -\> Blender procedural
geometry -\> mesh -\> runtime GPU animation. Rendering visualizes
mathematics and cannot redefine it.

Next foundational priority: turn/match state machine, then victory/loss,
then the 30 Prime catalog.
